# Product Scope — STEAL THE QUESTIONS

## Core loop
1. Answer beginner questions.
2. Earn question cards.
3. Build decks from owned cards.
4. Enter a duel with exactly 10 selected cards.
5. Each player answers the opponent's 10 questions under a short timer.
6. The winner steals exactly 1 card from the opponent.
7. Ranking is based on total cards owned.

## Fixed limits
- 1,111 questions total.
- 10 cards per duel.
- 5 decks per player.
- 2 decks free.
- 3 additional decks from Weekly Pass.
- One purchase product in the initial release: Weekly Pass.
- Arabic + English.
- Android + iPhone.
- Firebase, not VPS.

## Out of scope for the first build
- No characters.
- No worlds.
- No currencies.
- No equipment.
- No chat.
- No real-time character combat.
- No large social system.
- No question authoring system in the first visual prototype.

## Build order
1. Visual constants.
2. Card presentation.
3. Question + 3 answers + timer.
4. Answer feedback and transitions.
5. Duel flow.
6. Steal flow.
7. Cards and decks.
8. Firebase backend.
9. Weekly Pass and leaderboard.
10. QA, release build, and store preparation.
